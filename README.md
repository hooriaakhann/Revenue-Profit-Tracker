---

## Business objective

The dashboard is designed to answer practical performance questions quickly:

- How much profit is the business generating?
- Which categories and sub-categories contribute most to performance?
- How does profit change month by month?
- Which payment modes are used most frequently?
- Where are sales or profit concentrations forming?

The emphasis is on **business interpretation**, not only chart creation.

---

## Dashboard capabilities

| Area | Analysis |
|---|---|
| **Executive KPIs** | Profit, quantity, orders and sales summary |
| **Trend analysis** | Month-by-month profit movement |
| **Product performance** | Top-performing sub-categories |
| **Category mix** | Sales distribution across product categories |
| **Payment behavior** | Contribution by payment mode |
| **Decision support** | Fast identification of stronger and weaker business areas |

## Analytics workflow

```text
Raw Business Data
       ↓
Power Query Cleaning
       ↓
Data Modeling
       ↓
DAX Measures / KPIs
       ↓
Interactive Visual Analysis
       ↓
Business Interpretation
```
